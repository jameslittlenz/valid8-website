Trust Framework Reference Architecture

Commissioned by the Government Digital Delivery Agency (then the Department of Internal Affairs), Valid8 Advisory led the co-design, drafting and consultation of the Digital Identity Services Trust Framework Reference Architecture, a practical blueprint for putting the Digital Identity Services Trust Framework Act 2023 into operation.

The co-design process involved over 40 organisations across the public and private sectors through regular workshops held in person and online, whiteboard sessions, draft review and consultation, and collective contribution and endorsement.

The publicly available document defines the roles, trust model, assurance levels, data model and standards needed to issue, hold and present digital identity credentials securely, with accessibility, inclusion and privacy built in. It aligns with international standards and practice (ISO, W3C, IETF, OpenID and the EU) to support cross-border interoperability.

The reference architecture comprises nine chapters, annexes and supporting implementation guidance. It is the common reference point for everyone building digital identity services in Aotearoa New Zealand.
