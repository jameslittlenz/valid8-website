About Valid8

Many advisors stop at strategy. Our point of difference is execution.

We are a specialised advisory firm focused on enabling organisations and public services to deliver meaningful digital service and economic reform.

We partner with clients to implement customer-led approaches that deliver real outcomes across diverse stakeholder environments, whether navigating complex regulatory constraints or operating without established legislative frameworks.

We know the complexities of digital identity and verifiable credentials

Our deep understanding of Digital Identity and Verifiable Credentials stems from direct involvement in national policy development, ISO-standards implementation, international trust framework design, and cross-jurisdictional interoperability initiatives. We have navigated the technical, regulatory, and stakeholder coordination challenges that define successful digital identity adoption.

This includes leading Australia's first digital wallet rollout to assessing ecosystem readiness across Europe and the United States. This practical experience equips us to evaluate the diverse maturity levels and institutional contexts across jurisdictions, allowing us to tailor solutions and strategies while enabling international interoperability.
